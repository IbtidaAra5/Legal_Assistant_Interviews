# Interview Study: Explainable Legal Assistant for Bangladeshi Law

This repository contains the materials for the formative interview phase of our Final
Year Design Project, "Design and Evaluation of an Explainable Legal Assistant for
Bangladeshi Law to Mitigate LLM Hallucinations." We interview two groups, laymen (UIU
motorbike riders and shuttle drivers) and legal experts (lawyers and law students), to
understand their everyday problems, past experience with AI chatbots, trust, and
expectations. Findings will inform the design of our RAG-based Bangla legal assistant.
