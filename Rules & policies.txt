QUESTION RULES & Policies
- Neutral, non-leading, one idea per question, open-ended first, then probes.
- Laymen: simple spoken Bangla, no legal jargon. Experts: legal terms allowed but stay
  neutral.
- Past real experiences before hypotheticals.
- Never ask laymen directly about their own rule-breaking; use "you or people around
  you" framing.
- Do not quiz anyone on legal knowledge. Do not ask experts to review our dataset in
  this interview.
- Laymen opening script must include a reassurance line (important for shuttle drivers,
  who are university employees): answers are voluntary, confidential, and will not
  affect their job or studies.
- Experts: remind them not to share client or case details.
